How To Play

Survive from 12 AM to 6 AM. Monsters wander the building and try to reach your office.

  • Cameras — pull up the monitor (bottom button) to watch the rooms and track the monsters.
  • Doors — close the left / right door to block a monster that's right outside. Closed doors and lights drain power.
  • Lights — flick the door lights to check the blind spot just outside each door.
  • Power — you have limited power each night. Run out and you're defenceless in the dark…
  • Daniel only moves while you're not watching him on the cameras. Keep an eye out.
  • Watch Pirate Cove — if you ignore it, something will come sprinting down the west hall.
