How To Play

Last from 12 AM to 6 AM. The entertainers wander the building after close and they all end up at your office.

  • Look around by moving the mouse to either side of the screen (or A / D).
  • Cameras: swipe up, touch the bar at the bottom, or press Space. Swipe down to drop the monitor.
  • Lights show who is standing outside a door (Z / C). Doors keep them out (Q / E).
  • Power drains faster for every door, light and camera in use. At 0% you sit in the dark.
  • Daniel only moves while nobody is watching him on the cameras.
  • Check Pirate Cove. If the curtain is open and empty, shut the left door.
  • Esc pauses. M mutes.
To apply call: 1-555-BDAY-DAN
